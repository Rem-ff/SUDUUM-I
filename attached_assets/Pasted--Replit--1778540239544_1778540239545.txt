# BRAND DIRECTION

SUDUM should feel like a modern Saudi space initiative with a long-term vision, not a casual online community and not a governmental entity.

The tone of the website must feel:

* ambitious
* modern
* intelligent
* minimal
* future-oriented
* credible

Avoid language that makes SUDUM sound small, temporary, or amateur.

Do NOT describe SUDUM as:

* “just a community”
* “fan page”
* “space club”
* “early stage hobby project”

Instead, position SUDUM as:
“A growing Saudi initiative focused on building a more connected and accessible space ecosystem.”

The website should create the feeling that SUDUM is building toward something bigger in the future.
